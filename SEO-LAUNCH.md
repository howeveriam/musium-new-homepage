SEO launch checklist

Development remains noindex, nofollow until final testimonial content and musium.org launch.
Canonical and sitemap target https://musium.org/. On launch: enable index/follow on production, preserve noindex on preview hosts using host-specific Cloudflare rules, redirect www to the chosen canonical domain, verify Search Console and submit sitemap.xml. Preserve old URL redirects after checking existing site URLs. Test actual deployed mobile performance and optimize images if needed. Do not add ratings or unverified business address details to structured data.
